## What I've built

### [VisaPass](https://visapass.ai)

Software for visa businesses to manage clients and prepare UK student and visitor visa applications.

I built the product from scratch: architecture, frontend, backend, agent workflows, deployment and security. We are currently onboarding partners.

VisaPass grew out of Enrola's visa pathway. We developed it into a separate product focused on the day-to-day work of visa businesses.

### [DevTender](https://devtender.ai)

A platform focused on development-sector tenders.

I built the product and co-founded it with colleagues who have also worked within the UN system. Our experience with procurement informs the problems we are working to solve.

### [Enrola](https://enrola.ai)

A platform helping students explore universities and programmes and navigate the enrolment process.

I built most of the product, including integrations that make Enrola's services available through ChatGPT and Claude using the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
